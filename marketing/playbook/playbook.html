1

The plan in one page

Goal: a business makes its first invoice on Kora within a day of seeing a reel, and pays within 30 days.

CAs are the multiplier: one CA can bring dozens of client businesses. Owners bring volume.

What the market shows

  • Competitors use creators, not studio ads. Vyapar's Facebook and Instagram promotion features creators talking to camera about leaving pen and paper behind. Copy the format, not the words.
  • Short wins. Viewers finish short videos far more often (Vidyard data, via Vidico: 65% finish videos under one minute). Aim for 20 to 35 seconds.
  • First 2 to 3 seconds decide it. Hook with a problem the viewer feels ("Payment kab aayega?"), then show the product solving it, end on the result.
  • One problem per reel. Thirty seconds is not enough for three features.
  • Micro and nano creators give better value than one big creator: higher engagement for the money.

The legal rules for influencer posts in India

ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India) Influencer Guidelines, updated April 2025. Both Kora and the influencer are responsible.

RuleWhat it means for Kora
Disclose any "material connection"Money, a free plan, a discount or any perk counts. A creator using Kora free in return for a post must still disclose.
Label clearly: #ad, Ad, Advertisement, Sponsored, Collaboration or PartnershipPut it at the start of the caption. Not in the bio, not after "more", not hidden among hashtags.
Label on the video itselfVideos up to 15 s: label on screen at least 3 s. 15 s to 2 min: on screen for one third of the length (a 30 s reel needs it for 10 s). 2 min or longer: for the whole section that promotes Kora.
Platform label tooInstagram "Paid partnership" and YouTube "Includes paid promotion" are required by the platforms and should be used in addition to the creator's own label.
Same language as the contentA Hinglish reel needs a label Hinglish viewers understand ("Ad", "Paid partnership").
Advice needs qualificationsFinancial or tax advice must come from a qualified person (for example a CA) who states the qualification upfront. Creators without one may only share general product information, such as "this is how I make my bill".
No false or unprovable claimsNo made-up customer numbers, ratings or savings, and no "files your GST return" (Kora does not file returns).
AI or virtual presentersMust say upfront that they are not a real person.
That is why scripts 7 to 10 are for CA creators only. They give GST guidance, so the creator must be a practising CA and show it ("CA [Name] · ICAI member") on screen and in the caption. Owner scripts 1 to 6 contain no tax advice: they show the product.

Put these in every creator contract

  • Deliverables (format, length, number of drafts), posting date and time, and both labels as above.
  • Kora approves the final cut before posting; you can ask for edits or removal if a rule is broken.
  • Usage rights: Kora may run the reel as a partnership ad for 60 days (agree the fee).
  • No other invoicing-app promotion for 30 days before and after (optional, costs more).
  • Payment terms (50% on approval, 50% after it is live for 7 days), tracking link and code.
  • Only claims from the brief; the creator actually uses the app on camera.

Instagram and YouTube posting rules, timing and hashtags

Instagram Reels

  • 9:16, 1080 × 1920. Reels can run up to 3 minutes; keep to 20–35 s.
  • Hook in the first 1–2 seconds; captions on; original video, no other app's watermark.
  • At most 5 hashtags (Instagram's 2026 cap); 3–5 specific ones. Avoid #reels, #explore.
  • Say the keywords out loud and on screen ("GST bill", "invoice", "UPI"); Instagram reads both.
  • Collab post: creator invites @kora as co-author, so it appears on both profiles.
  • Paid partnership label required for branded content; add Kora as partner.
  • Partnership ads: with the creator's permission, boost their reel from Kora's ad account.
  • Trial Reels: test a hook on non-followers before posting to everyone.
  • Music: a business account should use Meta Sound Collection (cleared for commercial use in Reels, Stories and ads), not popular film songs.

YouTube Shorts

  • 9:16, up to 3 minutes; best results usually 15–35 s.
  • 2–4 relevant hashtags (the first ones show above the title).
  • Tick "Includes paid promotion": YouTube adds a disclosure label at the start.
  • Put the link in the pinned comment and the description.
  • YouTube Audio Library music is fine on YouTube, but it is licensed for YouTube only, not for Instagram.
  • Shorts run on their own recommendation system; most views come in the first 24–36 hours.

5 · Influencers (continued)

Where to find them

  • Search Instagram and YouTube for "GST bill kaise banaye", "dukaan business tips", "CA life", "small business India"; note creators whose comments come from real shop owners and CAs.
  • Ask CAs you know who they follow; ask existing users.
  • Before paying, ask for last month's Insights: reach per reel, audience cities, ages, and the share of followers in India. Engagement on recent reels matters more than follower count.

Ways to pay

  • Fixed fee per reel (most common), 50% on approval and 50% after 7 days live.
  • Fee + referral: a lower fee plus a payment for every business that becomes paying with their code (for example Rs 300 each). Good for CA creators.
  • Barter only (free plan): fine for nano creators who really use it; still needs the #ad label.

The process, step by step

  1. Send the brief: the script page from this PDF, the do-not-say list, the labels, the link and code.
  2. The creator signs up, makes a real invoice on camera, and films in their own style; the script is a guide, not a reading.
  3. You approve the draft within 24 hours; check the labels and claims.
  4. They post as a Collab with @kora and the Paid partnership label at the agreed time.
  5. Kora reposts to Stories, pins a comment with the link, replies to every comment in the first hour.
  6. After 7 days: compare views, saves, link clicks, sign-ups, first invoices and paid plans per creator.

Do not let creators say

  • "Kora files your GST return" or "no CA needed".
  • Any number of users, ratings or "lakhs of businesses".
  • "Free forever, everything free" (WhatsApp sending is paid).
  • Anything about a feature they did not actually use on camera.
