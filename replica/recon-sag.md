## Buyer and model

CA-friendly desktop software for tax professionals. Sold as one-time installation plus yearly updation, with a demo and installation process. G1 describes SAG as "segment-first tax and GST software to professionals". The home page mentions "60K client base" and "500+ brands".

## Prices seen on G1

| Genius (income tax and TDS suite) | Rs 15,000 | Rs 7,000 |
| Gen IT | Rs 7,000 | Rs 4,500 |
| Gen TDS | Rs 6,000 | Rs 3,500 |

**Bible discrepancy.** The bible gives Gen GST as Rs 5,000 plus Rs 2,000 and "All In One" as Rs 15,000 plus Rs 7,000. On G1, Rs 15,000 plus Rs 7,000 is Genius, not the All-In-One GST product, and I did not see any Gen GST price. The bible figure for All In One may be a mislabel. Confirm on the live Buy Now or Price Calculator page before Kora's pricing leans on it. The home page also says SAG "updated the new price list of some software", so all prices may have moved.

## Product map on the Gen GST menu (G1)

E-Filing (GST return filing), e-Invoice ("one place for GST e-invoice"), e-Way Bill, "E-filing, Invoicing, E-waybill" bundle, GST E-Filing with Website, All-In-One GST with Website (a CA website bundled with the software). Also a CA Portal that builds website themes for CAs.

## Features reported by reviewers, not by SAG (G3)

User-friendly interface, Excel bulk import, cloud access across multiple clients, regular updates, e-invoice with IRN, bulk e-way bills, ITC register. These come from review-site summaries, not from anything I opened.

## Screens and flows

Not reachable without the demo or trial. Inferred jobs: import client data, reconcile, generate GSTR-1, 3B and 9, push e-invoices, bulk e-way bills. Confidence low. A CA-friendly design implies multi-client switching and bulk import, both worth Kora's accountant seat.

## Why this matters for Kora

The bible says the first buyer might be a business led by its CA. SAG shows the CA is already a paying software buyer, but spends once, installs on a desktop, and serves many clients. Kora's accountant seat (read and export) is the thin edge of that wedge. A CA who manages 30 clients wants one login that switches businesses, which the bible puts at v1.5 (multi-business). That ordering is worth testing with the CAs in beta.

## Out of scope for Kora

Income tax and TDS return filing, payroll, ROC and MCA filings, XBRL, share-transfer agent, low-code platform, CA website builder, biometric attendance. Each is a separate product.

## What cannot be cloned

Filing-grade connections to GSTN and income-tax portals, regulatory certification, the 60K client base.

## News box on G1 worth noting

- NPCI postponed UPI merchant discount rates on some payments until after Diwali, with a rollout expected in the second week of November (dated 13 Oct 2026). This is a trade headline on a competitor's site, not an NPCI notice. Check NPCI directly before the pricing model depends on it.
- A note that GST e-invoicing may extend to services imported under reverse charge. Also a headline, unverified. Relevant to Kora's reverse-charge flag.
